# Earlier AI notes from X · 2–4 October 2026

Inside AI · published-post archive

These notes were already shared on X. The dates below distinguish the original announcement from my post. This archive does not present them as new announcements today.

## Copilot can interact with desktop apps

Announced 1 October 2026; posted 2 October 2026.

GitHub's public preview brings desktop clicks and typing to Copilot CLI and the Copilot app on Windows and macOS. The feature is off by default, and incorrect actions remain possible. That opens a route for workflows in apps without an API; it does not establish that every app can be controlled reliably. [Original announcement](https://github.blog/changelog/2026-10-01-github-copilot-can-now-interact-with-desktop-apps/) · [My X post](https://x.com/Skywalkerlyzv/status/2106005263987994967).

## Gemini 4 Argon: output capacity and access are different questions

Announced 30 September 2026; posted 3 October 2026.

Google's announcement raises the output-token limit from 64K to one million. That is output capacity, not a statement about the context window or guaranteed reliability. The initial rollout is to trusted cybersecurity defenders through Fairwind. Wider paid API and Google AI Ultra access is planned without a public date in the announcement. [Google announcement](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/gemini-4-argon/) · [My X post](https://x.com/Skywalkerlyzv/status/2106225304515875044).

## Bonsai World: a robot can practise before reaching the field

Announced 2 October 2026; posted 3 October 2026.

Bonsai says Bonsai World turns satellite imagery into structured 3D simulations with conditions such as dust, animals and changing terrain. Gemini interprets the imagery into a map; Bonsai's own world model supplies the simulation. It is a way to train and evaluate before deployment. Simulated performance still needs to be checked in the real environment. [Bonsai announcement](https://bonsairobotics.ai/news/bonsai-robotics-unveils-bonsai-world-to-accelerate-physical-ai-across-rugged-environments/) · [My X post](https://x.com/Skywalkerlyzv/status/2106380722160431589) · [Connected explanation](https://x.com/Skywalkerlyzv/status/2106382212296036378).
