Indic data: keep the equations and the source trail

Okay, this is pretty cool. They're checking what survives translation, right down to the equations 👇

NVIDIA and AI4Bharat's new Indic data work keeps a trail from each translation back to its English source. A 12-language workflow also repairs translations that score poorly.

The planned corpus is about 2.8T tokens across 22 languages. Still marked "releasing soon", so this is the write-up to read for now.

https://huggingface.co/blog/nvidia/scaling-indic-pretraining

FLUX 3 Image Klein: give the model a layout

Ooh, this is the bit I like: give the model a layout, then ask it to make the image 😄

FLUX 3 Image Klein takes bounding boxes in the prompt. Handy when the subject needs to sit left and the title needs breathing room.

Tiny gotcha in the Diffusers guide: [y_min, x_min, y_max, x_max], scaled 0–1000. Y comes first. Still inspect what it generates.

Guide by Aritra Roy Gosthipaty, Sayak Paul and Linoy Tsaban:
