Ai2 budgets GPU time to make shared clusters easier to use

Ai2 changed how its researchers get GPU time. Its 9 October writeup explains why.

Everyone setting their job to HIGH priority had made the old priority levels useless. Some researchers kept idle jobs running so they could debug without another long wait.

The new system gives teams budgets of GPU time. A fair share scheduler tracks recent use over a seven day window. Jobs declare the minimum runtime they need to make progress. After that protected window, the scheduler can preempt them and requeue resumable work.

Ai2 reports that teams received 98% of the GPU hours they were owed during a 30 day test. Cluster occupancy stayed at 98%. Debug jobs' p90 queue wait fell from two hours to 30 seconds. Those are the reported deployment results; the simulation used different workloads and numbers.

There was a cost too. Preempting interactive sessions meant people lost state they had to rebuild by hand. Restorable sessions are planned work.

Checkpointing matters a lot when fair access depends on being able to resume.
