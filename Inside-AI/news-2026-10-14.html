A compressed debugging trace still needs its file paths and exact errors. Those are details the next tool call may use.

Source: https://www.langchain.com/blog/building-long-running-agents-with-scaledown-and-deep-agents

Gemma scam detection: a two-stage on-device pipeline

A lightweight classifier screens messages first. Gemma takes a closer look at suspicious conversations.

That's the two-stage pipeline Google DeepMind and GovTech describe for on-device scam detection. It's fine-tuned on local tactics; the 14 October write-up says app rollout will be phased and conversation access will require user consent.

False alarms on ordinary local chats would matter here too.

Source: https://deepmind.google/blog/on-device-ai-scam-detection-mddi/

Original post: https://x.com/GoogleDeepMind/status/2110190101707858039

Announced pipeline, phased rollout; no independent detection performance or blanket privacy guarantee.

Molmo2Fish: describing a tracking correction

Molmo2Fish lets a researcher try a tracking correction by describing the mistake.

Kai Van Brunt adapted Molmo 2 to sonar video of salmon. Ai2 says the prototype can fix some tracking errors after natural-language guidance. It's still undeployed and the strongest initial tracks were harder to improve.

The initial track still needs to be available when comparing a correction.

Source: https://allenai.org/blog/molmo2fish Code: https://github.com/tidalove/molmo2fish

Original post: https://x.com/allen_ai/status/2110094370523197873

Research prototype, not deployed; corrections remain incomplete and dataset-specific.
