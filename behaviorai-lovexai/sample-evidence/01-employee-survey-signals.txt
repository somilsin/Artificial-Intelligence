Source: Global AI Transformation Pulse Survey — Q1 2025
Respondents: 2,847 employees across 40 countries
Conducted by: People Analytics team, Fortune 500 client

SURVEY FINDINGS — AI UPSKILLING INITIATIVE (WAVE 2)

Completion vs. Application Gap:
- 84% of employees have completed at least one AI module in the LMS.
- Only 11% say they have used an AI tool to complete a real work task in the past 30 days.
- 73% say they "understand the concept" but "don't know how to start" applying it to their job.

Fear and Psychological Safety:
- 61% say they are afraid of making a mistake with AI in front of their manager.
- 54% say they feel "watched" when experimenting with new tools at work.
- 48% say their manager has never mentioned AI in a 1:1 conversation.
- 39% say they believe AI will be used to monitor their performance, not help them.

Manager Behavior Signals:
- Only 19% of managers have modeled AI tool usage in a team meeting.
- 67% of managers say they feel "unequipped" to coach their teams on AI adoption.
- Managers who have used AI tools themselves have teams with 3.4x higher AI adoption rates.

What Employees Say They Need (open text, top themes):
- "Show me how to use it for my actual job, not a fake example." (mentioned 812 times)
- "I need to practice without it going on my record." (mentioned 634 times)
- "My manager needs to use it first before I will." (mentioned 509 times)
- "One nudge at the right moment would help more than 10 courses." (mentioned 441 times)

LMS Completion vs. Behavior Change (6-week post-training audit):
- Employees who scored 90%+ on AI quizzes: 83% still not using AI tools on the job.
- Employees who received a peer recommendation to try a tool: 47% used it within 3 days.
- Employees who received a manager-assigned practice task: 58% used the tool within 1 week.

Constraints noted by HR and Legal:
- Individual AI usage data cannot be stored or shared without explicit employee consent.
- Behavioral nudges must be opt-out, not mandatory.
- No individual performance scoring based on AI tool adoption rate.
- Must support 6 languages: English, French, German, Mandarin, Spanish, Arabic.
- Must integrate with Microsoft Teams and Outlook — no new app installs permitted.

ROI requirement from transformation lead (Greg):
- Evidence of measurable behavioral change within 90 days.
- Success defined as: employee uses AI tool unprompted, on a real task, without being told to.
