# Manager Listening Sessions — AI Transformation Programme
**Date:** March 2025  
**Facilitator:** Softway Consulting  
**Participants:** 34 people managers across APAC, EMEA, and North America  
**Format:** 60-minute structured conversation, 6–8 managers per session

---

## Theme 1: Managers don't know what behavioral change looks like

> "I told my team to use AI more. But I have no idea if they actually are. I can't see their screen all day."
> — Regional Manager, APAC

> "The LMS tells me who completed the course. It doesn't tell me if anything changed."
> — Operations Lead, EMEA

> "I'd love to coach my team on this but I haven't figured it out myself yet. How am I supposed to help them?"
> — People Manager, North America

**Observed pattern:** 91% of managers define success as LMS completion, not behavioral change. None of the 34 managers spontaneously described success in terms of employee behavior on the floor.

---

## Theme 2: The knowing–doing gap is real and managers feel it

> "My team aced the quiz. Then I watched one of them spend 45 minutes formatting a report manually that AI could have done in 3 minutes. They just didn't connect the dots."
> — Senior Manager, APAC

> "Learning and doing are completely different. The course happens in a separate tab. The work happens in Teams and Outlook. Those two worlds never meet."
> — Transformation Champion, EMEA

**Observed pattern:** Managers consistently describe a disconnect between the training environment and the real work environment. Training lives in the LMS. Work lives in Teams, Outlook, and SAP. The two are never bridged.

---

## Theme 3: Fear is the biggest blocker, not skill

> "People are scared. They think if they use AI and it produces something wrong, it goes on their record."
> — HR Business Partner, EMEA

> "One of my best performers told me she'd rather do it manually than risk looking incompetent with a new tool in front of the client."
> — Client Services Lead, North America

> "The fear is not about AI replacing them. The fear is about being judged while they are learning."
> — Learning & Development Lead, APAC

**Observed pattern:** Psychological safety is the #1 blocker of AI adoption. Employees are not resistant to AI — they are resistant to being visibly bad at it in a high-stakes environment.

---

## Theme 4: What managers want from an AI coaching tool

- A signal that tells them which employees are stuck — without surveillance.
- A way to celebrate adoption without making non-adopters feel judged.
- A tool that helps employees practice in a low-stakes environment before going live.
- Nudges that feel human and contextual, not robotic and generic.
- Something that works inside Teams — they will not ask employees to open another app.

---

## Constraints confirmed by managers:

- Must not store individual AI usage metrics without consent.
- Must not create league tables or public adoption scores.
- Any coaching nudge must be optional and dismissable.
- Managers want to see team-level signals, not individual scores.
- Must work in Microsoft Teams. No new app installs.
